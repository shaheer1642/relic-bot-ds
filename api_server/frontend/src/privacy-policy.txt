##Discord Bot (Gauss Prime)##

Gauss Prime is the Discord bot for AllSquads. It runs the squad finder, Warframe worldstate trackers, market and relic lookups, and account and role features in Discord servers.

What we store:
• Your Discord user ID, linked to your AllSquads account. This account holds your verified in-game name, platform and bot settings (such as ping and auto-leave preferences).
• Your squad activity, such as the squads you join, squad history and reputation earned from completed squads.
• Your tracker subscriptions, meaning which worldstate alerts you want to be pinged for and in which channel.
• Messages and attachment links you send inside the private squad threads that the bot creates. These are stored so the same squad conversation can be shown to squad members on the AllSquads website and in matching threads on other servers.
• In some servers, the time a temporary role was assigned, so the bot can remove it automatically later.

How we use Discord data:
• Server members: The bot reads server membership and roles to give verified users their platform role, rank roles and nickname (set to their in-game name), to welcome new members in partner servers, and to check staff roles.
• Online status (presence): The bot only checks whether you are online, idle, do not disturb or offline. It uses this to skip pings while you are on Do Not Disturb or offline if you chose that in your settings, and to remove you from open squads when you go offline if you turned on auto-leave. Online status is never saved to our database. We do not read your activities, Rich Presence, custom status or devices.
• Message content: The bot reads messages in squad channels that server admins have set up, in the squad threads it creates, and in staff report or event-submission channels. Recruitment messages in squad channels are turned into squads and then deleted from Discord, and their text is not saved to our database. The bot also replies when a message contains a relic name (for example "axi a1") with that relic's drops and prices, and nothing from those messages is stored. In a dedicated dictionary channel, words are sent to a public dictionary service to look up their definitions.
• Your Discord data is never sold, never used for advertising, and never used to train machine learning or AI models.

How long we keep it:
• Account data, squad history and stored squad thread messages are kept for as long as your AllSquads account exists, or until you ask us to delete them.
• Tracker subscriptions are kept until you unsubscribe.

How to delete your data:
You can ask for your data to be deleted by opening a ticket or messaging staff in the Warframe Squads Discord server (https://discord.gg/346ZthxCe8), or by emailing shaheer1642@gmail.com. We will delete your linked account data, tracker subscriptions and stored squad messages.
